Hakomi therapy & mentoring sessions

Hakomi is a mindfulness-based somatic psychotherapy that supports healing, self-understanding, and transformation through the body.

What is Hakomi?

Rooted in mindfulness and compassionate awareness, Hakomi gently brings attention to unconscious patterns, beliefs, emotions, and protective strategies that may be shaping your experience and the way you relate to yourself and the world.

This work honours the body’s innate wisdom and natural capacity for alchemy and healing – creating space for deeper connection, integration, and lasting change. On the deepest level, Hakomi supports a reclamation of who you truly are.

In our sessions

In our sessions, we invite a slowing down. You’ll be guided with mindfulness to listen and attune to what is unfolding in your present experience – through sensations that arise in the body, emotions, thoughts, impulses, and memories. Within a supportive and compassionate space, we explore the patterns held in your body, heart and mind that may be limiting you from living fully and authentically. All parts of you and your experience are welcome here, explored with curiosity and care.

Rather than trying to fix or force change ourselves – Hakomi invites transformation to unfold organically, helping you reconnect with yourself and the deeper truth of who you are. Our sessions will support you in accessing and living from your most embodied, authentic and liberated expression.

Hakomi and mentoring sessions are offered in person in California, and online.
