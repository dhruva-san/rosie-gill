Meditation & mindfulness coaching

I offer 1:1 sessions and also group courses. Whether you’re completely new to meditation, or have an established practice and are looking for support and guidance, these sessions are designed to meet you where you are.

Together, we explore mindfulness and meditation as embodied practices that can support greater presence, clarity, compassion, and connection in daily life.

My approach weaves together mindfulness, somatic awareness, Buddhist psychology, and nervous system regulation — helping you deepen not only your understanding of meditation, but your relationship with yourself.

In our sessions, we may explore

  • Mindfulness and insight meditation (Vipassana), which includes “The 4 foundations of Mindfulness”: mindfulness of the body, emotions, mind states and “open awareness”.
  • Shamatha method (mindfulness of breath and concentration practices).
  • Loving-kindness and compassion practices (Metta).
  • Somatic grounding and self-resourcing tools.
  • Working gently with difficult emotions, thoughts, and inner patterns.
  • Mantra practices.
  • Buddhist psychology and contemplative teachings.
  • The Bramaviharas and how they can be cultivated in your own life.
  • Integrating mindfulness practice into everyday life.

The Buddha taught that much of human suffering arises through grasping, aversion, restlessness, fear, and disconnection. Through meditation and mindful awareness, we begin to meet our experience with greater curiosity, steadiness, and compassion.

Over time, this practice can support a deeper sense of ease, emotional resilience, self-understanding, and connection to life!
