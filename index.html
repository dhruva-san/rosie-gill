About Rosie

My work is devoted to the somatic path of awakening – supporting you to reconnect with yourself, your body, your soul purpose and your innate wholeness.

Together, we can tend to the places in your body, heart, and mind that are asking to be felt, seen, and held. In our sessions, we slow things down and bring gentle awareness to emotions, patterns in the body, and beliefs in the mind – so they can be met and integrated. From here, true transformation naturally unfolds.

Through this work, I support you in living a life rooted in love – with greater ease, connection, freedom and intimacy with life.

I’ve trained in Hakomi therapy through the Hakomi Institute, and in mindfulness and meditation with the Mindfulness Training Institute. I also hold a First Class BSc (Hons) in Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture from the University of Westminster.
