About

Hi, I’m Rosie. I’m a somatic practitioner and guide based in California.

I’m trained in Hakomi therapy, mindfulness and meditation coaching, and Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture (qualified in England). I also facilitate women’s embodiment work and circles.

My work is devoted to the somatic path of awakening – supporting you to reconnect with yourself, your body, your soul purpose, and your innate wholeness.

Together, we tend to the places in your body, heart, and mind that are asking to be felt, seen, and held. In our sessions, we slow things down and bring gentle awareness to emotions, patterns in the body, and beliefs in the mind – so they can be met and integrated. From here, true transformation naturally unfolds.

Through this work, I support you in living a life rooted in love – with greater ease, connection, freedom and intimacy with life.

I offer a safe, supportive space where you can explore whatever is present for you, at your own pace. Clients often share that they leave sessions feeling clearer, more connected to their bodies and hearts, and more at home in themselves.

I offer Hakomi and mentoring sessions (both in person and online), mindfulness and meditation coaching, and women’s embodiment work, including regular circles.

I’ve trained in Hakomi therapy through the Hakomi Institute, and in mindfulness and meditation with the Mindfulness Training Institute (MTI). I also hold a First Class BSc (Hons) degree in Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture from the University of Westminster (UK). I am a member of the British Acupuncture Council (BAcC), the UK’s largest professional regulatory body for acupuncture. I am also trained in Tui Na (Chinese bodywork), Reiki, and holistic massage.
